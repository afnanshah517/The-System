THE SYSTEM: Fitness Quests (installable app)

Files: index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js and 4 icon .png files. Keep them all together in one place (no sub-folders).

iPhone steps (GitHub Pages, free):
1. Unzip in the Files app. 2. Sign up at github.com and make a new PUBLIC repository.
3. Tap uploading an existing file, choose ALL the files, then Commit changes.
4. Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > / (root) > Save.
5. After 1-2 minutes open https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/REPO-NAME/ in Safari.
6. Share > Add to Home Screen. Open it once online; after that it works offline.

GPS and offline mode need the https link. Progress is saved on your phone for that address.
To update later: re-upload changed files and change CACHE in sw.js (e.g. system-fitness-v2).
